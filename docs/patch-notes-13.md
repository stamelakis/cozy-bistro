#Patch 13  23-11-2026
@Tester
Guest Wishes

🎂⚡ Your guests now ask for things
Every few minutes one guest gets a little gold badge over their head. Tap it in time and they pay you extra:
• 🎂 "It's my birthday" — shows while they eat. Tap it within 20 seconds: +$6 on the spot.
• ⚡ "I'm in a hurry" — shows when they sit down. Tap it to promise a fast plate. First plate on the table within 60 seconds of the badge appearing: +$10. Stirring the pot (🥄) helps.
• A happy wish guest also rates you a little higher, and a regular gets an extra loyalty heart.

Good to know
• Miss a wish and nothing bad happens — the badge just goes away.
• Wishes only come while you are in your restaurant, and only when you have the staff to serve that guest (waiter + chef, or a barman at the bar).
• One wish at a time, with a pause of a few minutes in between. You'll find the money in the 📓 Ledger as "Guest wish".
