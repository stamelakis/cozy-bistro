#Patch 12  12-08-2026
@Tester
The Command Bar Update

🎛 One bar to rule them all (PC)
The scattered menus are gone — everything now lives in a single bar across the top:
• Navigation (zoom/rotate/home) on the left · every button in the middle — big tiles for the essentials (Build, Recipes, Upgrades, Pantry, Staff, Decor), compact ones for the rest · timers and the 🔔 on the right · floor buttons hang neatly beneath.
• Build, Recipe Menu, Chat, and the new 🌐 Players list open as dropdowns from the bar and tuck away when closed — nothing floats around the screen anymore. (You can still drag/resize any panel after opening it.)
• The left sidebar is now a pure dashboard: money, stats, daily goals, crate.
• Mobile keeps its own layout — nothing changed there.

🧹 Pick up your litter!
Litter no longer quietly recycles itself for free money. It now piles up on your sidewalk — actual bottles, banana peels, newspapers, pizza slices — until YOU tap the ♻ on each piece for $2. Keep your street clean; it's your reputation lying on the pavement.

🍳 New furniture — Prep Counter
Tier 2, $600. Each one shaves 8% off EVERY dish's cook time (two count, −16% max). Your first real lever against a slammed kitchen.

💬 Feedback button
Found a bug? Have an idea? There's now a Feedback button in the game — every note lands directly on my desk and I read all of them.

🏆 Champions wear their medal
Weekly-challenge winners now get a 🏆 painted on their door plaque — visible on your own sign and to everyone who visits you.

⚖ Big fairness fixes
• No staff, no economy — a restaurant can no longer take orders without a waiter, cook without a chef, or auto-buy groceries without an errand helper (and Auto-shop ON). Previously an unstaffed restaurant kept buying ingredients for meals it could never serve — that money leak is dead.
• Your restaurant will never silently reset again. The season-reset marker moved from your browser to your ACCOUNT — new device, new browser, cleared cache, new domain: your restaurant follows you. (This bug ate two restaurants during beta. If it got yours, ping me for a refund.)
• Dishware is now reserved and settled 100% server-side — the definitive end of plate counts drifting.

🐛 Fixes & polish
• Daily-goal claiming works on the first click, every time — no more eaten clicks or repeat "collects" (the reward was always paid only once, but the button misbehaved).
• The ledger's +$2 drip is now correctly labeled "Recycling ♻" — it was showing as "Furniture refund".
• Auto-shop OFF now genuinely means nobody shops automatically.
• Waiters prioritize washing as clean dishes run low — no more running dry mid-rush.
